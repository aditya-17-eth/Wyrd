# Product specification

Audience: outdoor event organizers in Pune who want a predefined financial trigger for heavy rainfall, and invited underwriters willing to lock capital against that obligation. This prototype offers no actual insurance protection.

Standard offer: 200 mUSDC coverage / 25 mUSDC premium. The competing manager's demonstration offer is 200 / 30. Neither price is actuarially validated. Buyers choose a single-use quote whose exact dated event specification, vault, price, expiry, cutoff, and capacity can be inspected. A standard event limits cumulative purchased coverage per buyer to 200 mUSDC and outstanding coverage across all vaults to 1,000 mUSDC.

Core journey: faucet → collateral approval → invited deposit → start underwriting → publish quote → buyer approval/purchase → full reservation + separate escrow → archive evidence → authorized commitment → permissionless finalization → permissionless policy settlement → end issuance → close settled epoch → redeem.

Screens: Explore, Buy protection, Underwrite, My policies, Resolution explorer, Demo operator. Financial values come from chain reads. Missing RPC/deployment/evidence states remain unavailable rather than use fabricated figures. Synthetic events have prominent labels and distinct IDs; live and synthetic vaults cannot mix.

Excluded: flight/crop/shipping cover, permissionless factual dispute resolution, AI pricing, prediction-market prices, ML training, tradable shares, partial liability withdrawals, leverage, production insurance, and mainnet deployment. Multiple invited vaults are implemented; genuine customer demand and sustainable underwriting compensation remain hypotheses.
